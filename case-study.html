Product design case study

Designing a hospital command center around shared situational awareness.

CareFlow was framed as a command product: one place for clinicians, operations leads, and handoff teams to see the same truth under pressure.

Problem

Critical decisions were split across queues, calls, notes, and room status updates.

UX strategy

Make status, risk, owner, and next action visible before the next escalation.

Outcome

A command center experience with 100 demo records, login, local database actions, and a working backend path.

Design principles

Built for scanning, explaining, and acting.

01Reduce triage overload with a visible acuity model.
02Show missing context before it becomes a handoff problem.
03Use plain language for status, owner, and next action.
04Connect data analytics to process improvement.
